# Snitch-Fashion-Sales-Analysis-Using-Excel and Power-BI

## Project Overview

This project turns raw fashion sales data into actionable insights through data cleaning and dashboard analysis. It highlights top-performing categories, sales distribution, and key trends, helping businesses understand what drives revenue and where to focus for growth.

## 🛠️ Tools

• Microsoft Excel

• Power BI

• DAX Functions 

• Slicers

• KPI cards

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

• Removed duplicate and irrelevant records to ensure data accuracy.

• Handled missing values in key fields

• Standardized text data.

• Converted data types (dates, numeric values) into proper formats.

• The Cleaned excel file is loaded into Power BI

• Created measures for key metrics such as total orders, total units sold, and total sales for analysis.

• Cleaned and structured data for better analysis and visualization

• Verified dataset consistency before building the dashboard



## Key Insights

• Total Sales By Segment(Pie-chart)
Which segment contributed more sales B2B or B2C.

• Total Unit Price Vs unit Sold By Products(Scatter Plot)
Based upon the unit price which products sold more.

• Total Sales By Year (Line Chart)
Identified which year had high sales.

• Total Sales By city(Bubble Map)
Finded out  which city has high sales (Ex.Mumbai,Ahamedabad,etc..)

• Total Sales By Product Category(clustered column chart)
Which product category has high sales Ex.T-shirt.

• Total Sales By Product category and Segment(clustered column chart)
Total sales distribution across product categories and customer segments highlights which combinations drive the highest revenue.


## Slicers and KPI cards

• KPI cards are used for Total sales,Total units sold and Total orders.

• Slicers used for product category


## Recommendatios

• Focus marketing and inventory on top-performing segments (B2B/B2C).

• Increase stock and promotion for high-revenue categories.

• Optimize pricing strategy for low-selling high-price products.

• Expand business in high-performing cities and target similar markets.

• Improve performance of low-selling categories through offers or bundling.

## Key Business Insights

• B2C/B2B segment dominates overall sales contribution.

• Certain product categories (e.g., T-Shirts) drive the majority of revenue

• Higher-priced products show lower sales volume, indicating price sensitivity

• Sales show clear yearly growth/decline trends

• Revenue is concentrated in a few major cities (e.g., Mumbai, Ahmedabad)

## Conclusion
The analysis reveals key sales drivers across segments, products, and cities, providing a clear direction to optimize pricing, inventory, and market focus for improved revenue growth.
